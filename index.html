How It Works

Browse the items below and select I'll Provide This for anything you'd like to contribute.

1. Find an item → 2. Sign up → 3. Check your email → 4. Bring it or ship it to school

After signing up, you'll receive a confirmation email with the item details, quantity, condition, and example link (if available), so you'll have everything you need when you're ready to shop or donate.

Items may be purchased from any store. Items marked New or Used are welcome to be secondhand as long as they are clean and in good condition.
